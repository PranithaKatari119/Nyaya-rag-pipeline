How do I request a contract review?

All contract review requests must be submitted through the Legal Request Portal together with supporting documentation.

What is the standard turnaround time?

The standard turnaround time for contract reviews is 5 business days from the date of submission.

What is the expedited turnaround time?

Urgent contract reviews are prioritised and completed within 1 business day where sufficient information is provided.

Who do I contact for an urgent contract review?

Contact the Legal Operations Manager, Sarah Bennett, at sarah.bennett@ncslegal-sample.com or +91-9876543210 for urgent contract review requests.

What happens after approval?

Approved contracts are stored in the central contract repository and retained in accordance with the records management policy.
